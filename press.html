01

Papers

Nine papers, each with a DOI. Full bibliography on ORCID.

  1. 2026 · Zenodo

    The Agent Social Contract

    The foundational paper. Agent Passport, Human Values Floor and Beneficiary Attribution, the three layers the protocol grew from.

  2. 2026 · Zenodo · SSRN

    Monotonic Narrowing for Agent Authority

    The invariant that keeps delegation chains safe. Authority can narrow as it passes down, never widen.

  3. 2026 · Zenodo

    Faceted Authority Attenuation

    A product lattice model for how scoped authority narrows across delegation hops, facet by facet.

  4. 2026 · Zenodo

    From Access to Derivation

    Governing what a persistent agent may learn and reuse from what it observes, not only what it may do.

  5. 2026 · Zenodo

    Physics-Enforced Delegation

    Binding hardware attestation to delegation chains. Seven experiments on IBM Quantum hardware.

  6. 2026 · Zenodo

    Governance in the Medium

    Why the unit of agent governance is the population and the medium that carries its actions, not the single agent.

  7. 2026 · Zenodo

    Cognitive Attestation

    Signed commitments over which interpretable model features were active during a decision, tested against Llama 3.1 8B.

  8. 2026 · Zenodo

    The Evidence-Safety Gap

    Signed receipts show a procedure was followed. They do not show the effect was safe. Five classes of what they miss.

  9. 2026 · Zenodo

    Plausibly Wrong

    Peer-voted retention kept a worse shared memory than random selection in 4 of 5 replicates. Pre-registered, with kill conditions.

    An approved lab for testing agent authority across implementations. I proposed it and maintain it.

    Co-lead of the RFC-189 conformance cases.
